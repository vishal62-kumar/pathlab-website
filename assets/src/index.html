Crucial Preparation

10-12 Hours Fasting is Essential

As highlighted on our official testing protocol, accurate evaluation of your Lipid Profile (Cholesterol, Triglycerides) and Blood Sugar/Glucose strictly requires an overnight fasting window.

Why is fasting necessary?

Food consumption elevates blood triglycerides and glucose temporarily. Fasting establishes your true biological baseline for early detection.

What You CAN Do

  • Drink normal plain water freely to stay hydrated.
  • Take essential prescribed heart or BP medications with water (unless advised otherwise by your physician).
  • Ensure 7-8 hours of peaceful rest the night before.

What to AVOID

  • No tea, coffee, milk, or juices in the morning before blood draw.
  • Avoid heavy oily dinner and alcohol for at least 24 hours prior.
  • Refrain from intense early morning workouts before sample collection.
